# MattBread Arcade — Mini Pack 04: Arcade Ops

Games 31–40 are ten original top-down arcade shooters inspired by classic wave-survival and twin-stick arcade games. They do not use Call of Duty or other games' characters, maps, sounds, or assets.

## Game lineup
31. **Dead Zone** — Hold off the undead for as many waves as you can.
32. **Neon Breach** — Survive security bots in a glowing sci-fi arena.
33. **Last Stand** — Stay mobile, manage ammo, and survive the rush.
34. **Biohazard Run** — Escape infected creatures and collect supply drops.
35. **Mech Mayhem** — Fight armored drones and powerful boss units.
36. **Night Ops** — Keep moving through a dark arena and avoid getting surrounded.
37. **Mutant Swarm** — Battle fast-moving mutants in escalating waves.
38. **Cyber Siege** — Defend the core from waves of hostile machines.
39. **Outbreak Protocol** — Collect data chips and survive until extraction.
40. **Boss Rush: Arena** — Face elite enemies and prove you can survive the arena.

## Controls
- **WASD / arrow keys:** move
- **Mouse:** aim
- **Hold mouse button / Space:** fire
- **R:** reload
- **P:** pause
- **Mobile:** directional buttons and FIRE

Each game has its own high-score save. All ten titles use the shared original top-down shooter engine in `play.html`.
